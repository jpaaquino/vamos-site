Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what data the Vamos Espanha! app (the “App”) processes, why, who it is shared with, and your rights. It describes what the App actually does today. If that changes, we will update this page. The Portuguese version is the primary one.

1. Controller

Joao Paulo de Araujo Aquino, NIF/NIE Z1454771A, Calle Pascual i Genis, 15 5-17, 46002 Valencia, Spain. Privacy contact: joaopaulo@acedigitallabs.com.

2. Summary

  • You can use the App without an account. Your progress then stays only on your device.
  • An account is optional and saves your progress to the cloud. You can sign in with email and password, an email sign-in link, Google or Apple when available on the platform. Social sign-in providers may send your email and, if available, your name.
  • We do not use advertising, ad tracking or crash-reporting tools, and we do not sell data. The App (iOS and Android) uses anonymous usage statistics, with no name, email or answers, to improve the course. In the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (or if the device region cannot be determined), they are collected only if you agree. In other countries, such as Brazil, they are on by default, with a notice in the App. Wherever you are, you can turn them off at any time in Configurações › Privacidade (Settings › Privacy).
  • Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google, with technical support from RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.

4. Processors

  • Supabase: database and authentication, hosted in the EU region (Ireland, eu-west-1). It also sends the confirmation and sign-in emails.
  • Google and Apple: optional social sign-in providers when available on the platform and if you choose to use those services.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): technical processing of purchases and subscriptions.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): payment, billing, renewals and refunds, under their own privacy policies.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and the web demo, and may log IP addresses in technical logs.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area (for example, to the USA) rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

5. Retention

  • Local data: until you delete the App or clear your browser data.
  • Cloud account and progress: while the account exists. Deletion in the App is immediate. Email requests are handled within 30 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Usage statistics: 90 days in Supabase, with a daily deletion routine. If you turn them off (or withdraw consent), collection stops immediately and the local queue is cleared; removal of events already sent is requested from Supabase on the next connection. To prevent resending, a pseudonymous record containing the random installation UUID and a hash of the deletion secret, with no account link, is kept for 90 days.
  • Technical logs: for the provider's retention period.

6. Deleting your data

Without an account: uninstall the App (or clear the site data in your browser) and local progress is removed from the device. With an account: in the App, open Configurações → Sua conta → Apagar conta (Settings → Account → Delete account). The account and its cloud progress are deleted immediately. To delete usage statistics, turn off Compartilhar estatísticas de uso (Share usage statistics) in the Privacy section of Settings. This stops collection, clears the local queue and requests removal of events already sent on the next connection; the deletion request stays pending until it can be sent. If you can no longer use the App, email joaopaulo@acedigitallabs.com. See How to delete your account. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

7. Minimum age

The App is meant for people aged 14 or over (the digital-consent age in Spain under the LOPDGDD), or the minimum age required in your country if that is higher. If you are younger, use the App only with your parent's or guardian's permission and supervision. If we learn that we collected a child's data without that permission, we will delete it.

8. Your rights (GDPR / LOPDGDD)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions. Email joaopaulo@acedigitallabs.com; we reply within one month. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

9. Brazil (LGPD)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and review of automated decisions (LGPD art. 18). You may also complain to the ANPD. The legal bases we use are contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest (including for usage statistics outside the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, which you can turn off at any time) and, for usage statistics in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, consent, which you may withdraw.

10. California (CCPA/CPRA)

You may know what personal information we collect, request its deletion or correction, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or “share” personal information (in the CCPA sense) and do not use sensitive personal information. Requests: joaopaulo@acedigitallabs.com.

11. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Database security rules mean each user can access only their own data. Passwords are hashed. No system is 100% secure: if a significant incident happens, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

If we make material changes, we will announce them in the App or on this page, with a new effective date.
