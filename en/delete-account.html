How to delete your Vamos Espanha! account

Updated October 18, 2026

In the App (recommended)

  1. Open Vamos Espanha! and tap the Configurações (Settings) gear, then Sua conta (Account).
  2. Tap Apagar conta (Delete account).
  3. Confirm with Apagar para sempre (Delete forever).

Deletion is immediate and permanent: we delete your account (email and password), your profile and all progress saved in the cloud. On this device, the App goes back to guest mode.

By email

If you can't use the App, email joaopaulo@acedigitallabs.com from your account address with the subject “Delete account”. We delete within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: account, profile, completed lessons, XP, streak, stamps, review items.
  • Kept: purchase records that tax law requires (held by Apple/Google and RevenueCat) and technical security logs until they expire.
  • Guest progress on your device: remove it by uninstalling the App.

Usage statistics

Usage statistics are anonymous and identified by a random device UUID, not your email. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland they are collected only if you agree; elsewhere they are on by default. You can turn them off at any time: turn off Compartilhar estatísticas de uso (Share usage statistics) under Configurações → Privacidade (Settings → Privacy). Collection stops immediately, the local queue is cleared, and removal of sent events is requested from Supabase on the next connection. To prevent resending, a pseudonymous record containing the random installation UUID and a hash of the deletion secret, with no account link, is kept for 90 days.

Purchases and subscription

The one-time course purchase creates no recurring charges. If you have an active monthly subscription, buying the course does not cancel or offset it. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. To stop future monthly charges, cancel it in the store: App Store (Apple) or Google Play. Refunds follow the store's rules.
