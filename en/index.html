Learn the Spanish of Spain, travelling with Lu

Vamos Espanha! helps Brazilians study the Spanish spoken in Spain with dialogues, audio and exercises explained in Brazilian Portuguese. The course has 117 lessons, including 21 educational lessons about vocabulary and everyday situations involving paperwork and services. Seven lessons are available to try for free.

🗺️ A trip across Spain

Madrid, Barcelona, Andalusia, Valencia, Galicia, the Basque Country, the Canary Islands: every region has episodes built on everyday situations.

⚠️ Same, close or trap?

Practise the Spanish–Portuguese false friends, like exquisito, embarazada and oficina.

📄 Paperwork vocabulary

21 educational lessons with words and situations about documents and services. The content is not professional advice and does not provide complete official requirements.

🎧 Audio from Spain

Every sentence has audio in peninsular Spanish, with vosotros, vale and ordenador.

🔁 Review your mistakes

What you get wrong comes back in later lessons until you get it right.

🛂 Passport stamps

Finish regions, earn seals and keep your daily streak going.

The app itself is in Portuguese (Brazil).

Inside the app

Course access
